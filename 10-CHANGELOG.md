# Path of Nine Heavens - Changelog

Every released version, newest first, in one format: NEW / CHANGED / FIXES.
Play any version in your browser from the versions page, or install its APK on Android.

---

## v0.2 - November 16, 2026

NEW
- Closed-door seclusion: pick 1 month to 100 years, projection card before you go, early stop at breakthrough gates, danger or end of lifespan, full report (You / People / Sect or clan / World / Market / Danger) when you come out.
- Random events: 171 events, ~1,000 text variants, shaped by your role, alignment, realm and crafts.
- Spiritual affinity: elements matter for spells and techniques; shops and teachers show it; very rare Heavenly Affinity (single element, x1.8); foes have affinity too.
- Karma and heart-demon trials: karma follows your alignment; major-realm breakthroughs from Nascent Soul up start a 3-5 trial heart-demon test first.
- Seven new crafts: formation, beast taming, spirit farming, geomancy, divination, puppetry, poison - ten crafts in all with alchemy, refining and talismans.
- Beasts: bond a cub; each later realm needs one catalyst (pill, rare material or demon core).
- Farm plots, mining puppets and guard puppets: crafts have loops of their own.
- World Market: one view of towns really selling, with real stock.
- Ranked pills: one clean Use button; a breakthrough pill you can brew per realm.
- Fate: a very rare event can rewrite a Mortal Root into an Immortal Root (35% success; failure can kill you).

CHANGED
- Secret realms: shorter trips and a themed report when you come out.
- Tribulation: lightning grows with how much heaven has noticed you; failing leaves lasting scars (slower qi, shorter life).
- Your cave turns away raids and beast tides; commissions kept in your save.
- Alchemy, refining and talismans follow the same learning and realm rules as the new crafts.
- The Creation screen shows the version.

FIXES
- Opening the game no longer fast-forwards time if you left mid-battle, mid heart-demon trial, on a report or with an event waiting.
- Shop, loot and battle text reads cleanly ("Bought: Iron Ore", "Requires: Array Flags").
- A raw label on the tribulation screen now reads "ally rests".

Save: a v0.1.33 save loads and keeps playing in 0.2. Balance is still being tuned.

## v0.1.33 - November 15, 2026

FIXES
- The clock now keeps up if the app sleeps in the background.
- Crash screen with a recover option instead of a blank page; auto-save every 10 seconds and when you leave the app.
- % rewards are capped by how long you've actually lived/idled - no more realm skipping.
- Family time actually passes game time now.
- Merit Hall duplicate entries gone.

CHANGED
- Gifts to friends/family/disciples have a 7-day cooldown.
- Social and family activities give ~3-4x idle - worth doing, not a shortcut.
- Chamber deals nerfed hard (12x to 2x), barter capped.
- Pill prices scale with realm - low-realm margin kept, high-realm capped.

## v0.1.3 - November 15, 2026

NEW
- Ventures board: Hunts, Escorts, Gathering, Mining (days + realm + risk shown before you go).
- Lucky road encounters: inheritance cave, wounded cultivator, hidden market.
- Ores + smelting: refine your own artifacts (incl. a flying raft usable from Qi Refining).
- More artifact sources: hunt drops, market stalls, wanderer favours, milestones.
- Milestones: 13-step chain from "first hunt" to Golden Core, rewards on claim.
- Quest menu: milestones + NPC quests + bounties + sect duties + family missions, all rewards claimed there.
- NPCs: 3x more people per city, random names/temperaments, 4 new roles, chatting can open random quests.
- Family: missions via "ask what troubles them" + share a meal, cultivate side by side.
- Realm tab: progress, bottlenecks, breakthrough chance, ladder of realms (secret realm on top).
- Sect tab: rank ladder, seasonal sect events, Merit Hall.
- Settings page: light/dark/device theme, text size, calm mode, game speed, offline progress.
- Character creation: gender choice + 6 avatars each.
- Powers ranking on the map: strongest first with rank numbers, arrows = climbing/falling.

FIXES
- Performance: map stopped redrawing every second, lag way down.
- Saves survive updates/downgrades; auto backup every hour; copy/paste backup in Settings.
